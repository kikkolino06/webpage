History

The Stanley Cup and the league behind it

The NHL was founded in 1917. The Stanley Cup is older: it was first awarded in 1893, which makes it the oldest trophy in North American pro sports.

The Original Six

From 1942 to 1967 the NHL had just six teams: Boston, Chicago, Detroit, Montreal, the New York Rangers and Toronto. All six still play today, and fans still call them the Original Six. In 1967 the league doubled to 12 teams, the first of several expansions that brought it to 32. The newest clubs are the Seattle Kraken (2021) and the Utah franchise (2024), now called the Mammoth.

Seasons without a champion

The Cup has gone unawarded twice. In 1919 the Final was called off during the flu pandemic, and in 2005 a labor dispute wiped out the entire season.
