Build a six-player NHL lineup for $50M and play a 10-week season.

Pick three forwards, two defensemen and a goalie. Each week your lineup plays three simulated games against a computer team. Most fantasy points wins the week.

It runs in your browser. No account, no email, no ads.

How a season works

  1. Draft your six

    Choose from 62 current NHL players. Stars cost more. You can take McDavid, MacKinnon and Kucherov together, but then your defense and goalie come from the bargain bin.

  2. Play a week

    Every player on both rosters gets three simulated games. Goals, assists, shots, blocks and saves turn into points.

  3. Tweak and repeat

    Swap players between weeks if someone goes cold. After week 10 you get a final record. Then start over and try a different build.

Roster rules

  • 3 forwards (F), 2 defensemen (D), 1 goalie (G).
  • Total price can't go over $50M.
  • Your lineup has to be full before you can play a week.
  • You can change players any time between weeks.
  • Your season saves in this browser, so you can close the tab and come back.

About the numbers. The prices and ratings in this game are our own estimates for balancing the draft. They are not real salaries or official NHL statistics, and the weekly results are a simulation, not real games. Teams are listed as of the start of the 2025-26 season, so a recent trade may not show up yet.

New to hockey? The short version

Positions

Forwards (centers and wingers) do most of the scoring. In this game they're your main source of goals and assists.

Defensemen protect their own net and start the attack. The best ones also rack up assists from the blue line, and they block a lot of shots, which counts here.

Goalies stop the puck. A goalie on a strong team picks up wins; a busy goalie on a weaker team makes more saves. Both can score well.

Quick strategy notes

Paying for one elite forward and filling the rest with mid-priced players usually beats spreading the money evenly.

An offensive defenseman can outscore a cheap forward because of the blocked-shot points.

Goals against cost a point each, so a cheap goalie can sink a good week. Don't leave yourself $3M for the net.

Results are random week to week, like real hockey. Judge your build over the full 10 weeks.
