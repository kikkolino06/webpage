New this season

Starting in 2026-27, every team plays 84 regular-season games instead of 82, and the preseason drops to four games. The change came with the collective bargaining agreement the league and players signed in 2025.

The salary cap also climbs to $104 million per team, up from $95.5 million last season.

From the history page

Florida went back to back

The Florida Panthers won the Stanley Cup in 2024 and again in 2025, beating the Edmonton Oilers in the Final both years. They were the first repeat champions since Tampa Bay in 2020 and 2021.
