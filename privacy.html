Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Short version: Rink Report has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Your game stays in your own browser.

What we store

Your team name, draft picks and season results are saved in your browser's local storage so you can come back to your season later. This data never leaves your device and we can't see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for your name, email or any other personal information. We don't use cookies, tracking pixels or analytics tools, and we don't sell or share data with anyone.

Third parties

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those fonts, Google receives standard request information such as your IP address and browser type. See Google's privacy policy for how they handle it.

The hosting provider that serves these pages may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operation. We don't use those logs to identify you.

Children

The site doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the project's GitHub repository.
